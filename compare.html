What Pocketmind does not do

Pocketmind cannot search the web, read images or make pictures, and phone-sized models are less capable than the large models that run on servers. It is not on Google Play, so it does not update itself.

Where the others do more

Layla adds characters with memory, more than 100 voices, image generation and plugins. PocketPal AI runs on iPhone too, has built-in benchmarking and is open source.
