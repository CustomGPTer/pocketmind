Privacy policy

Last updated 26 November 2026. This policy covers the Pocketmind app for Android (package uk.jj.pocketmind) and this website. Pocketmind is made by Jonathan Jackson, an individual developer in the United Kingdom, who can be reached by opening an issue on the project's GitHub page.

The short version

  • Your conversations are worked out and kept on your phone. They are never sent anywhere.
  • Pocketmind goes online only for tasks you start: downloading a model or the voice pack, searching for models, and checking for updates. It locks offline again afterwards.
  • There are no accounts, no servers, no advertising and no analytics.

What stays on your phone

Conversations, assistants and settings are kept in the app's private storage. Saved conversations, download plans and any Hugging Face access token are encrypted with AES-256-GCM using a key held in Android's Keystore. Temporary chats are never saved. Android's cloud backup and device transfer are switched off for Pocketmind.

Keeping and deleting your data

Delete any conversation or model in the app. Uninstalling Pocketmind deletes everything it holds, apart from backups you have saved elsewhere.

Your rights

Because the developer holds no data about you, there is nothing to request, correct or erase on a server. Everything is already in your hands.

This website

This site is hosted by GitHub Pages. It uses no cookies, no analytics and no scripts beyond the app list. GitHub may keep standard web server logs, such as IP addresses, for security, under GitHub's privacy statement.
